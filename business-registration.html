The foundation

Give your business a formal starting point.

Business registration generally involves forming an entity and filing the required information with the appropriate state authority. It can help establish the legal framework a business uses to operate, contract, and move forward.

The right approach depends on ownership, taxation, management, business goals, investment plans, operating location, and regulatory requirements. Mark Central Pro provides preparation support, not individualized legal or tax advice.

Plan with clarity

Start with the entity, then organize the next steps.

Formation documents, an EIN where applicable, and brand protection each serve a different role in the life of a business.

Entity considerations

Common structures, considered in context.

No structure is universally right. Consider professional advice for circumstances that call for it.

LLC

A limited liability company is often considered by owners who value a flexible management structure. Its suitability depends on the business and applicable rules.

Corporation

A corporation is a separate legal entity with formal ownership and governance features that may suit particular operating or growth plans.

C Corporation

A C corporation is one corporate tax classification that founders may evaluate when considering ownership, fundraising, and long-term business goals.

Where to register

State selection is part of the planning.

Delaware, Wyoming, and a founder’s home or operating state are commonly considered. The best fit depends on where the business operates, its ownership, regulatory needs, and future plans.
