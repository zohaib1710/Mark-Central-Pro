A trademark can be a word, phrase, symbol, design, or combination that identifies the source of goods or services and distinguishes it from others.

Federal registration may strengthen your ability to protect a brand name, logo, slogan, or other source identifier and can provide important public notice of your claim.

Trademarks generally identify the source of goods or services. Copyright protects original creative expression fixed in a tangible form.

Examples include visual art, photographs, musical compositions or lyrics, websites, motion pictures, scripts, screenplays, sound recordings, and other eligible original works.
