Information we collect

At Mark Central Pro, your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you access or interact with our website.

Personal information

When you fill out forms or engage in activities on the website, we may collect information such as your name, email address, contact details, and other relevant data you voluntarily provide.

Usage data

We may collect information about how the website is accessed and used, including IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages visited, and date and time of a visit.

Cookies and tracking

The website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve your experience, understand site usage, and support relevant communications.

Live chat

We use Tawk.to, a third-party service provider, to offer live-chat support. When the chat service loads or you use it, Tawk.to may process chat messages, contact details you choose to provide, IP address, device and browser information, cookies, and usage data. For more information, review the Tawk.to Privacy Policy.

How we use information

Information may be used to provide and maintain services, respond to inquiries, monitor and improve website functionality, personalize the experience, and communicate service-related or promotional updates where permitted.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may be disclosed to service providers that help operate the website, in response to legal requests or applicable law, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

Security of information

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Your rights and choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data. You may opt out of promotional communications and can control cookies through browser settings.

Children's privacy

The website is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

SMS terms of service

By voluntarily opting into SMS through a web form or other medium, you agree to receive messages from Mark Central Pro. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time by following the instructions in a message.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy by posting a revised version on this page. For questions about this policy or personal information, contact support@markcentralpro.com.
